Beta Maintenance Bug Log - Progress Save 2

I next tested the phase formula at the borders between 30-day periods. This
save adds the phase-boundary correction to the first two function fixes.

1. Weight changes were always zero for small calorie balances

While testing calculateWeightChange(), I originally divided two integers. A
balance such as 350 calories returned 0 instead of 0.1. I corrected the
calculation by dividing by 3500.0 so C++ uses floating-point division.

2. The -100 calorie boundary was put in the wrong category

My first condition used balance <= -100 for a deficit. The project definition
says -100 through 100 is balanced, so the -100 test failed. I changed the
deficit condition to balance < -100 and kept the balanced condition at
balance <= 100.

3. Day 30 was assigned to phase 2

I first calculated the phase with day / 30 + 1. That made day 30 start the
second phase. I changed the formula to (day - 1) / 30 + 1 and added tests for
days 30, 31, 60, and 61.
